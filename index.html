About

Hi! I'm Wending, a second-year undergraduate studying Applied Mathematics at the University of Liverpool. I’m also a researcher at PremiLab, where I work under the supervision of Qiufeng Wang.

My research interests include multimodal reasoning, AI for mathematics (AI4Math), and post-training. I’m particularly interested in how AI models can develop stronger reasoning abilities and tackle challenging problems. Driven by curiosity and a fascination with the unknown, I enjoy exploring new ideas and pursuing research that I find exciting.
